# Refund Policy

1. **Time Window**: Refunds are only allowed if the request is made within
   30 days of the order date. Anything older is automatically denied.

2. **Condition**: Physical items must be "unused" to qualify for a refund.
   Items marked "used", "opened", or "worn" are not eligible, except
   defective-item claims (not covered by this bot — escalate to human).

3. **Sale / Clearance Items**: Any item marked `isSaleItem: true` is
   final sale and non-refundable, regardless of condition or time window.

4. **Digital Goods**: E-books, courses, and other digital products are
   non-refundable once "downloaded" or "accessed". If condition is
   "not accessed", a refund is allowed within the 30-day window.

5. **Refund Amount**: The refund amount can never exceed the original
   price paid for the item.

6. **One Rule Fails = Deny**: If ANY of the above rules disqualifies the
   order, the refund must be denied. All rules must pass for an approval.
